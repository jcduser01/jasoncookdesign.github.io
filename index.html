Frequently Asked Questions

What's included?

Each project is tailored to your specific needs and can include wireframes, design specifications, user research, interactive prototypes, requirements documentation, competitive analysis, and more. I work with you to define the deliverables that will have the greatest impact on your business.

Which pricing model should I choose?

  • The Day Rate is ideal for short-term projects with well-defined, focused goals that require quick turnaround.
  • Outcome-Oriented Pricing is best for larger projects with clear, measurable objectives where value can be directly tied to results.
  • A Monthly Subscription is perfect for ongoing or evolving needs, offering continuous access to expertise and flexibility as your project grows.
